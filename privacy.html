My Vehicle Book – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026 · Developer: Indilab Studio

My Vehicle Book is a vehicle service log: service history, reminders and fuel economy. It works entirely on your phone. This policy explains what that means for your data.

What we collect

Nothing. My Vehicle Book has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

Your vehicles, records and photos

Vehicles, service and fuel entries, reminders, documents and the photos you add (vehicle, receipts, documents) are stored only inside the app on your phone. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling My Vehicle Book deletes all of it, so use Export backup if you want to keep a copy.

When you share a PDF report or a backup file, it goes only where you choose to send it, using your phone's share sheet.

Purchases

My Vehicle Book Pro is a one-time purchase handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only asks the store whether Pro has been purchased on your store account. The store's own privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Permissions

  • Camera & photos – only when you add a vehicle, receipt or document photo.
  • Notifications – service reminders, scheduled on your phone. You can turn them off at any time.
  • Internet – used only by the App Store / Google Play to process and check the Pro purchase.

Children

My Vehicle Book is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email indilabp@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 working days.

Lost Pro after reinstalling or changing phones? Open Settings → Plan or the Pro screen and tap Restore purchase while signed in to the same store account.
